# OPERATING AGREEMENT OF BROOKLYN INDUSTRIAL ARTS, LLC

This Operating Agreement (the "Agreement") of Brooklyn Industrial Arts, a New York limited liability company (the "Company"), is adopted by the members listed in **Schedule A** (the "Members") pursuant to **Section 417 of the New York Limited Liability Company Law**.

**ARTICLE I: FORMATION AND PURPOSE**

**Section 1.1 Formation.** The Company was formed by the filing of Articles of Organization with the New York Department of State.

**Section 1.2 Name.** The name of the Company is Brooklyn Industrial Arts.

**Section 1.3 Purpose.** The purpose of the Company is to operate a shared workshop for the fabrication of mixed materials. The Company shall be operated on a **cooperative basis**: democratically controlled by its Members, for the primary purpose of **reducing costs** and providing affordable access to tools and space, rather than for generating profit.

**ARTICLE II: MEMBERS AND MEMBERSHIP CLASSES**

**Section 2.1 Classes of Members.** The Company shall have two classes of Members:

**(a) Managing Members (Full Members).** The Managing Members are the owners of the Company with full voting rights. Each Managing Member shall have **one (1) vote** on all matters submitted to the Members. The initial Managing Members are the twelve (12) individuals listed in **Schedule A**.

**(b) Participating Members (Flex Members).** Participating Members are non-owners who pay fees for access to the Company's facilities. Participating Members have **no voting rights** and no ownership interest in the Company. Their rights and obligations shall be governed by a separate Access Agreement.

**Section 2.2 Admission.** The admission of a new Managing Member requires the **unanimous written consent** of all existing Managing Members. The admission of a Participating Member requires the approval of the Board.

**Section 2.3 Termination.** A Managing Member may be expelled for cause by a **three-fourths (3/4) vote** of the other Managing Members.

**ARTICLE III: MEETINGS AND VOTING**

**Section 3.1 Member Meetings.** An annual meeting of Managing Members shall be held for the purpose of electing the Board. Special meetings may be called by the Board or by one-third (1/3) of the Managing Members.

**Section 3.2 Voting.** Except as otherwise provided herein, all decisions of the Managing Members shall be made by a **simple majority** vote, with each Managing Member having one vote.

**Section 3.3 Supermajority and Unanimous Consent.**  
**(a)** The following actions require the affirmative vote of **three-fourths (3/4) of the Managing Members**: expelling a Managing Member.  
**(b)** The following actions require the **unanimous written consent** of all Managing Members: admitting a new Managing Member; imposing a new mandatory capital contribution or monthly expense on all Managing Members.

**Section 3.4 Entity Members.** A Managing Member that is a partnership or other legal entity shall hold a single vote on all matters submitted to the Managing Members, identical to the vote of an individual Managing Member. Such entity Member shall designate a natural person as its Voting Representative by written notice to the Board, signed by an authorized partner or agent of the entity. The Voting Representative shall cast the entity Member's one vote. The entity Member may change its Voting Representative at any time by written notice. The designation of a Voting Representative is not a proxy and does not affect the one-member, one-vote principle.

## ARTICLE IV: MANAGEMENT

**Section 4.1 Management by Board.** The day-to-day business and affairs of the Company shall be managed by a **Board of Managers** (the "Board"), which shall consist of **four (4)** Managing Members elected annually by the Managing Members.

**Section 4.2 Officers.** The Board shall designate a **President, Treasurer, Shop Manager, and Community Manager** from among its members to oversee specific operational areas.

**Section 4.3 Authority and Limitations.** The Board shall have the full power to manage the Company, except for those matters reserved to the Managing Members under **Article III**. The Board is specifically **prohibited** from taking the following actions without Member approval:  
**(a)** Dissolving the Company (requires a two-thirds (2/3) vote of Managing Members);  
**(b)** Amending this Agreement (requires a two-thirds (2/3) vote);  
**(c)** Spending more than **$200** on un-budgeted discretionary expenses in a month;  
**(d)** Taking any action that would cause the Company to cease operating on a cooperative basis.

**Section 4.4 Recall.** Any Manager may be removed by a majority vote of the Managing Members.

## ARTICLE V: CAPITAL, TAX, AND FINANCIAL PROVISIONS

**Section 5.1 Capital Contributions.** Managing Members shall make capital contributions as determined by the Board and approved by the Members.

**Section 5.2 Distributions.** The Company shall be operated on a non-profit basis. Any net surplus shall be used to reduce member costs, improve facilities, or be held in a collective reserve. Distributions to Managing Members shall be made only to the extent necessary to reimburse them for capital contributions or operating expenses they have advanced, and shall not be based on profit.

**Section 5.3 Tax Treatment.** The Members intend for the Company to be classified as a partnership for federal and state income tax purposes.

**Section 5.4 Dissolution.** Upon dissolution, after payment of debts, remaining assets shall be distributed to the Managing Members in accordance with their positive capital account balances.

## ARTICLE VI: MISSION PRESERVATION

**Section 6.1 Cooperative Principles.** The Company shall at all times be operated in accordance with the following principles: (1) democratic control by Managing Members on a one-member, one-vote basis; (2) non-profit operation for the benefit of members; and (3) preservation of affordable access to workshop facilities.

**Section 6.2 Amendment.** Any amendment to this Agreement that would alter the cooperative principles in **Section 6.1** shall require the **unanimous written consent** of all Managing Members.
