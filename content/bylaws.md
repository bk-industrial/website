# BYLAWS OF BROOKLYN INDUSTRIAL ARTS COOPERATIVE

## ARTICLE I: NAME, PURPOSE, AND OFFICES

**Section 1.1 Name.** The name of this corporation is Brooklyn Industrial Arts Cooperative, hereinafter referred to as the "Cooperative."

**Section 1.2 Purpose.** The Cooperative is organized under the New York Cooperative Corporations Law. Its primary purpose is to provide affordable and functional workshop space, tools, and a community for its members for the fabrication of mixed materials through cooperation. The Cooperative's goal is to reduce costs for its members rather than to generate profit. The Cooperative does not aim to own significant assets; expenses will be funded by member contributions and revenue generated by the Cooperative.

**Section 1.3 Offices.** The principal office of the Cooperative shall be located at 544 Park Ave, Ste 332, Brooklyn, NY, 11238, or such other place as the Board of Directors may designate.

## ARTICLE II: MEMBERSHIP

**Section 2.1 Classes of Membership.** The Cooperative shall have two classes of membership:

**(a) Full Members (Owning-Members).** Full Members are those who are tenants of the workshop space and who hold a full ownership interest in the Cooperative. Full Members have full voting rights and are entitled to one vote on all matters submitted to the membership. The initial Full Members are the twelve (12) individuals listed in **Schedule A** attached hereto.

**(b) Flex Members (Non-Voting).** Flex Members are individuals who are granted access to the workshop space and its tools under a separate access agreement. Flex Members pay fees that contribute to the Cooperative's operating expenses but do not hold an ownership interest and have **no voting rights** in the governance of the Cooperative. The number of Flex Members shall be determined by the Board.

**Section 2.2 Admission of New Members.**  
**(a)** The admission of any new Full Member shall require the **unanimous consent** of all existing Full Members.  
**(b)** The admission of a new Flex Member shall be approved by the Board of Directors in accordance with the Cooperative's access policies.

**Section 2.3 Termination of Membership.** A Full Member may be expelled for cause (e.g., a material violation of the Cooperative's Code of Conduct) only upon a **supermajority vote of three-fourths (3/4) of the Full Members** present at a duly called meeting. The expelled member shall be entitled to a refund of their capital contribution, if any, as determined by the Board and consistent with applicable law.

**Section 2.4 Transfer of Membership.** Membership interests in the Cooperative are non-transferable except as approved by the Board and the Full Members.

## ARTICLE III: MEETINGS OF MEMBERS

**Section 3.1 Annual Meeting.** An annual meeting of the Full Members shall be held each year for the purpose of electing Directors and transacting any other business that may properly come before the meeting.

**Section 3.2 Special Meetings.** Special meetings of the Full Members may be called by the Board of Directors, the President, or by written petition of at least one-third (1/3) of the Full Members.

**Section 3.3 Notice.** Written notice of any meeting, stating the place, date, and hour, and the purpose or purposes for which the meeting is called, shall be delivered to each Full Member not less than ten (10) nor more than sixty (60) days before the date of the meeting.

**Section 3.4 Quorum.** A majority of the Full Members entitled to vote shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business.

**Section 3.5 Voting.** Except as otherwise required by law or these Bylaws, all questions shall be decided by a **simple majority** of the votes cast by Full Members present in person or by proxy. Each Full Member shall have **one (1) vote**.

**Section 3.6 Supermajority and Unanimous Votes.**  
**(a)** The following actions shall require the affirmative vote of **three-fourths (3/4) of the Full Members**: expelling a member.  
**(b)** The following actions shall require the **unanimous consent** of all Full Members: adding a new Full Member; adding a new monthly expense category that materially affects member contributions.

**Section 3.7 Entity Members.** A Member that is a partnership, corporation, association, or other legal entity shall be entitled to the same single vote as any individual Full Member. Such entity Member shall exercise its vote through a natural person designated in a written notice signed by an authorized partner, officer, or agent of the entity and delivered to the Secretary of the Cooperative. The designated representative may be changed by subsequent written notice. The vote cast by the designated representative shall be the vote of the entity Member, and the entity Member shall not be entitled to any additional vote by virtue of having multiple partners or owners.

## ARTICLE IV: BOARD OF DIRECTORS

**Section 4.1 General Powers.** The business and affairs of the Cooperative shall be managed by its Board of Directors, which shall exercise all such powers of the Cooperative and do all such lawful acts and things as are not by statute, its Certificate of Incorporation, or these Bylaws directed or required to be exercised or done by the members.

**Section 4.2 Number, Term, and Election.** The Board shall consist of **four (4)** Directors, who must be Full Members. At each annual meeting, Directors shall be elected by a plurality of the votes cast by the Full Members for a term of one (1) year and shall serve until their successors are elected and qualified.

**Section 4.3 Officers.** The Board shall elect from among its members a **President, a Treasurer, a Shop Manager, and a Community Manager**. The Shop Manager and Community Manager shall oversee the day-to-day operations of the workshop and community, respectively. The Treasurer shall have custody of the Cooperative's funds and shall keep full and accurate accounts of receipts and disbursements.

**Section 4.4 Recall of Directors.** Any Director may be recalled, with or without cause, by a **majority vote** of the Full Members at a special meeting called for that purpose.

**Section 4.5 Specific Limitations on Board Authority.** The Board is expressly **prohibited** from taking the following actions without prior approval by the Full Members as specified:  
**(a)** Dissolving the Cooperative (requires a two-thirds (2/3) vote of Full Members);  
**(b)** Expelling a Full Member (requires a three-fourths (3/4) vote);  
**(c)** Amending these Bylaws (requires a two-thirds (2/3) vote);  
**(d)** Spending more than **$200** on un-budgeted discretionary expenses in any given month.

**Section 4.6 Board Meetings.** The Board shall meet at least quarterly. A majority of the Directors shall constitute a quorum. Actions of the Board shall be by majority vote of the Directors present.

## ARTICLE V: FINANCIAL MATTERS

**Section 5.1 Fiscal Year.** The fiscal year of the Cooperative shall end on [Date].

**Section 5.2 Capital Contributions and Fees.** The Full Members shall contribute to the capital and operating expenses of the Cooperative as determined by the Board and approved by the membership. Flex Members shall pay access fees as set by the Board.

**Section 5.3 Distributions.** The Cooperative shall be operated on a non-profit basis for the benefit of its members. Any surplus revenue shall be used to reduce member costs, improve the workshop, or be allocated to a collective reserve account as permitted by law. No dividends shall be paid on membership interests.

**Section 5.4 Dissolution.** Upon dissolution of the Cooperative, after paying or adequately providing for all debts and liabilities, the remaining assets shall be distributed to the Full Members in accordance with their capital accounts, or if not feasible, on an equal basis per member, consistent with the New York Cooperative Corporations Law.

## ARTICLE VI: AMENDMENTS

These Bylaws may be adopted, repealed, or amended by the affirmative vote of **two-thirds (2/3)** of the Full Members voting at a meeting held after due written notice setting forth the proposed action. The Board of Directors may also propose amendments for consideration by the membership.
